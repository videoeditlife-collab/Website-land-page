# they are opposite prospects for an editing service:
#

#                 are the sellable half.
#   filmmaking  - McKinnon's own half. Their editing IS the product, and
#                 several already have assistant editors. Worth having as
#                 market research and for editor-to-editor referrals, not for
#                 direct pitching.
#
