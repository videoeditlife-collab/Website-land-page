# Outreach email — creator long-form editing

Merge fields match the columns in `all_creators.csv`, so this can be mail-merged
straight from the CSV. `{Hook}` is the one field that has to be written by hand.

---

**Subject:** your {Cadence} upload schedule

**Subject (alt):** editing {Channel}

---

Hi {FirstName},

{Hook}

I'm JB — I edit long-form YouTube, and I lead every cut with sound. Most
retention problems I see aren't pacing problems, they're audio problems: the
room tone drops, the music sits wrong under a beat, and people leave without
knowing why.

You're posting {Cadence} and that's the part I'd want to protect. Uploading at
that rate usually means editing is eating the week that should go into
filming.

If it's useful, I'll edit one of your videos — a real one, start to finish, no
charge, no obligation to use it. You compare it against your own cut and decide
if there's anything there.

Worth a quick call?

JB
JB Medias — {WebsiteURL}

| Field | Source |

| `{Channel}` | `Display Name` |

| `{Hook}` | Manual — see below |

## Writing the hook

One or two sentences naming something only a viewer of that specific video
would know. This is the line that decides whether the email gets read, and it
cannot be automated — the CSV knows subscriber counts, not what happens at
4:12.

Good:
> The Santorini video — you cut the music out completely when you walked into
> the cave church and let the reverb carry it. That's a choice most travel
> channels don't make.

Bad (generic, reads as a template):
> Love your content! Your videos are amazing and I'd love to work together.

Budget ten minutes per channel: open the two most recent videos, skip to the
middle, write down one specific thing.

## Sending notes

- **Where to send.** "Email in YouTube Bio" means an address is listed, not that
  it was captured — YouTube requires sign-in and a CAPTCHA to reveal it. For the
  73 channels with Instagram, a DM often lands better anyway.
- **Volume.** Send in batches of 10–15, not 91. A cold send from a new domain to
  ninety addresses at once is what spam filters are built to catch.
- **Follow-up.** One follow-up after 5–7 days, then stop. Reply to your own
  thread, keep it to two lines.
- **The free edit is the offer.** It's concrete, it demonstrates the work
  instead of describing it, and it costs a creator nothing to say yes to. Don't
  bury it under credentials.
