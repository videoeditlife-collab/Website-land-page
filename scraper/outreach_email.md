### The cadence line cuts both ways

For `monthly` and `biweekly` channels it lands — slow output reads as a
bottleneck. For the 55 channels posting **weekly**, "the edit is eating your
week" is wrong and they'll know it. Use instead:

> You're posting weekly and holding it, which is the hard part. The question is
> what you'd make if the edit wasn't yours to do.

### Sending

- Batches of 10–15. Ninety cold emails from one domain in a day is the pattern
  filters are built to catch.
- "Email in YouTube Bio" means an address is listed, not captured — YouTube
  needs a sign-in and CAPTCHA to reveal it. 73 of the 91 have Instagram, which
  is often the faster route.
- Three touches then stop, as above.
- **Make the breakdown before you send.** The ask is "want me to send it over?" —
  if a reply comes back the same hour and nothing exists, the sequence dies at
  the one point where it was working.
